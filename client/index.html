Controls & game rules

Keyboard: WASD / arrows to move, mouse to aim, click to fire, Space to dash, hold Shift to boost, R to reload.
Touch: drag the movement stick and aim stick; dash, boost and reload buttons are on screen. Change layout in Pause.
Collect C, S and R pads for cannon, shotgun and rapid fire. Empty magazines reload automatically. Green + pads restore armor.
